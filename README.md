# Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis

March 2025 – September 2026

## Project overview
This project examines 10.28 million bike-share trips to understand how annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic differently, and how those insights can inform a marketing strategy to encourage casual riders to purchase annual memberships.

The analysis indicates that annual members use the bike-share system more frequently during weekdays and exhibit commuting-related riding patterns. Casual riders, meanwhile, take longer trips, ride proportionally more on weekends, and more frequently return bicycles to their starting stations.

These findings suggest an opportunity to develop marketing campaigns that emphasize the convenience and potential value of annual membership for casual riders who already use the system for recreational or occasional transportation.

## Business question
How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently, and how can these differences inform strategies to convert casual riders into annual members?

## Tools used

BigQuery (SQL), Google Sheets, Excel, data visualization tools, and ChatGPT assistance.

## Data preparation
Google BigQuery was used to prepare and analyze the dataset, with Google Sheets supporting the review and visualization of query results.

The preparation workflow included combining monthly records, checking data types, investigating missing station values, and identifying invalid trip durations.

## Key findings
The analysis identifies five main findings:

1. Annual members account for most recorded trips, representing 63.2% of ridership.
2. Casual riders take longer trips across every season and throughout the week.
3. Casual ridership is more weekend-oriented, with longer average durations on Saturdays and Sundays.
4. Members demonstrate commuting-related hourly patterns, particularly on weekdays.
5. Casual riders favor lakefront stations and same-station returns, consistent with greater recreational usage.
   
Taken together, these findings suggest that Cyclistic's two customer categories exhibit meaningfully different riding patterns.

## Recommendations
1. Target casual riders at popular recreational destinations
2. Launch seasonal and weekend-focused campaigns
3. Promote membership as an everyday transportation option
