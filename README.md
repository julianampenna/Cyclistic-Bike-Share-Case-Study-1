# Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis

March 2025 – September 2026

## Project overview
A brief explanation of your Google Data Analytics capstone project and its business objective.

## Business question
How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently?

## Tools used

BigQuery (SQL), Google Sheets, Excel, data visualization tools, and ChatGPT assistance.

## Data preparation
Summarize the merging of monthly tables, treatment of invalid trip durations, and handling of missing values.

## Key findings
Highlight your results about seasonal riding, trip duration, weekend behavior, commuting hours, and station preferences.

## Recommendations
1. Target casual riders at popular recreational destinations
2. Launch seasonal and weekend-focused campaigns
3. Promote membership as an everyday transportation option
